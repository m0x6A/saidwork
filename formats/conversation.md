**Date:** {{date}}

## Summary
One bullet per topic covered, in the order they came up: the topic in bold, then one or two
sentences on what was said about it. Every topic in the write-up below must appear here.

## Topics
Write up the conversation topic by topic, one `###` heading per topic, in the order the topics
were first raised. When the conversation returns to an earlier topic, add what was said to that
topic's section instead of starting a new one.

Under each heading, rewrite what was said as clear prose. This is a rewrite, not a summary:
keep every point, argument, example, anecdote, number, name, question and disagreement, along
with who said it when the speakers can be told apart and the reasoning behind each view. Drop
only filler and literal repetition. If something is unclear or was left unresolved, say so
rather than smoothing it over.

### Topic
What was said about it.
